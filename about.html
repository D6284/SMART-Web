Why SMART TECH exists, and where it's going.

A technology education and innovation institution being built from Cameroon, for Cameroon — and beyond.

Our story

It started with a simple desire.

SMART TECH began with a simple desire: to use technology and education to equip young people and solve real problems in the community. What started as a small effort to teach practical technology skills is growing into a structured institution built around four working parts — Academy, Solutions, Innovation Lab and Community.

Our mission

Skills and solutions that hold up in the real world.

To equip young people and organizations with practical technology skills and innovative digital solutions that solve real-world problems.

Our vision

A recognized technology institution, built in Cameroon.

To become a recognized technology education and innovation institution in Cameroon, empowering the next generation of technology professionals while developing solutions that transform communities and organizations.

Long-term vision

From an online community to a physical institution.

SMART TECH will begin online and grow progressively into a physical technology institution with training centers, technology laboratories, professional programs, software products, partnerships, and innovation initiatives.
